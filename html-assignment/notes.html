HTML Tags

Title tag
This is the title of your website shown on the tab bar.
Blockquote tag
Creates a standalone quote on a webpage.
Subscript and Superscript (sub and sup) tag
Allows the user to put in subscript or supercript text like with dates.
Abbreviation (abbr) tag
Makes you able to abbreviate a word and have the full meaning pop up when you hover over it.
Table Head (thead) tag
A heading tag specifically used as the header of a table.
